### PhD Candidate in Statistics (ABD) | Quantitative Researcher & Data Scientist

Building probabilistic deep learning frameworks and extreme value models to anticipate rare, high-consequence "black swan" events across complex physical and financial systems.

### Overview

I am a Quantitative Researcher and Statistics PhD Candidate (ABD, expected Dec 2026) at the University of Missouri-Columbia, where I also earned my M.S. in Data Science and Analytics.
My research operates at the intersection of extreme value theory, probabilistic forecasting, and complex stochastic systems. By combining attention-based time series models and 3D Conditional Variational Autoencoders (CVAEs) on fluid dynamic and spatiotemporal datasets, I design architectures that perform precisely where standard distributional assumptions break down.

I specialize in deploying complex theoretical models to solve high-stakes operational challenges:

* Amazon: Architected a production-grade probabilistic system on AWS to simulate edge cases and estimate tail-risk across the North American delivery station network.
* Walleye Capital & Quantitative Research: Built end-to-end NLP pipelines scraping 100,000+ unstructured documents to extract thematic market signals and actionable intelligence.
* McDermott and CB&I: Pioneered and scaled the first quantitative procurement analytics functions

Robust data engineering and scalable ML are central to everything I build. I program fluently in SQL, Python, and R, leveraging PyTorch, LightGBM, and Bayesian networks for model deployment. To keep my feature engineering and tuning skills sharp, I actively compete in predictive modeling challenges on Kaggle.
