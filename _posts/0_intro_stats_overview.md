# Introduction to Probability and Statistics

This is a series of blog posts designed to help students learn the basics of statistics. The 14 blog posts linked here will cover the basic information that a Stats 1 / Stats 2 class will cover. Notably, each blog will introduce a topic, provide the mathematical framework (where applicable), and then demonstrate how to perform these tasks in MS Excel. 

The following pages should not be considered as a complete review of the topics, but merely a summarization of what you will be learning in class.
